Controls

Controller: left stick steers. Right trigger drives, left trigger brakes. Or use the right stick like a stick radio: up to drive, down to brake. X calls the marshal, Y changes camera, Start pauses, A starts.

Touch: left thumb slides sideways to steer, right thumb slides up to drive and down to brake. Each stick centres where your thumb lands.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD, R for the marshal, C for camera, Esc to pause.

No reverse: the car has a racing ESC, as in the sim. The brake only stops the wheels, and letting off is a light drag brake. If you get stuck, call the marshal.

In the air: throttle lifts the nose, brake drops it. The spinning wheels react against the chassis, as on a real car.

What is simulated

The training sim's own car and tracks: MuJoCo 4.0.1 at 2 ms steps, the truggy physics (real="truggy"): soft suspension with 30% sag, one 0.6 N·m-per-wheel motor through limited-slip diffs, heavy wheels, an ESC brake with no reverse, the elliptic friction cone, and tires that lose a quarter of their grip once they slide. The servo takes 0.12 s per 60°, steering has the gate car's 0.4 expo, the battery sags, and 15 ms pass between your command and the wheels.

The loops are Phase 4.6's validation (10000–10019) and practice (100000–100019) loops, rebuilt from the sim's heightfield. The test loops are left out.

The AI cars

Ports of the project's hand-coded teacher, reading the sim's true state. Each draws its physics from the training range; yours is nominal.
